What precedes each streak (the asymmetry)

Loss streaks → head-fakes

14 of 23 (61%) of all loss streaks follow exactly one isolated win. Long loss streaks (≥5) are even more lopsided: 9 of 12 follow a single W.

...LLLLLLLW | LLLLLLLLLL ← 9-L streak after 1 W
...LLLLLLLW | LLLLLLLLL ← head-fake pattern repeats

Win streaks → droughts

6 of 8 win streaks of length ≥3 emerge from a long loss streak (≥4). Wins need momentum to build — the regime change is preceded by a longer drought.

...LLLLLLLLL | WWWWWW ← W×6 after 9-L
...LLLLLLLLLLL | WWWW ← W×4 after 11-L

Auto-rule (bot now follows sim20)

Shipped late afternoon — a toggle in the v2 dashboard (follow_sim_signal) that automates the on/off rule. Bot stays in running state; the toggle gates entries based on sim20's most recent resolved trade.

  • ▸ Sim20 wins → next v2 entry fires normally.
  • ▸ Sim20 loses → v2 logs a skip with reason "sim20 signal OFF" and waits.
  • ▸ Open positions always resolve normally — only new entries are gated.
  • ▸ Sim service unreachable → fail-safe to OFF (don't trade on a missing signal).
  • ▸ 3-second result cache so 5 per-asset checks per window = 1 actual HTTP fetch.
  • ▸ Resets to OFF on bot restart — explicit re-arm each session.

Implementation lives in bot_v2.py:_sim20_signal_on() with the entry check in _evaluate_cheap_contract. The 61 paper sims never have this setting set, so their behavior is unchanged — sim20 remains the unfiltered signal source.

Answers to May 11's six open questions

Q1 · Auto-rule edge overnight eroding

v2-with-rule: −$41.46 over 53 trades (WR 18.9%). Sim20 unfiltered same span: +$128.53 / 129 trades, +$51 scaled to $2 bets. Rule under-performed unfiltered by $93 overnight. Lifetime since migration still positive (rule +$129 vs scaled-sim20 +$75) — entire edge was earned May 11 afternoon.

Q2 · sim20_hourgated lead evaporated · don't ship

First 46 trades: +$317 @ 54% WR (the snapshot). Trades 47–199 (overnight): −$93.82, $−0.61/t, WR 21.6%. Lifetime now $+1.12/t vs sim20 baseline $+1.04/t — essentially tied. The "+$235 lead" was a 46-trade hot run on the old bad-hour set (2/4/9/10/11/12). Don't ship this hour gate.

Q3 · cheap_ct_no_sol per-trade roughly tied

Lifetime (200 trades): no_sol $+0.97/t vs sim20 $+1.02/t (all assets) or $+0.77/t (ex-SOL). Overnight: no_sol $+1.22/t vs sim20-ex-SOL $+0.68/t. No_sol pulls ahead of the ex-SOL comparator by $0.21/t lifetime / $0.54/t overnight, but loses to sim20-all-assets on the period because SOL was net break-even on the overlap window.

Q4 · SOL multi-day EV noise — hour-of-day matters

4,295 SOL trades cohort-wide: $+0.19/t. Cross-sim mean $+0.09/t · 95% CI [−$0.12, +$0.31] straddles zero. May-11: +$0.51/t, May-12: −$0.32/t — sharp regime flip. Hours 2/4/9/10/11/22/23 are catastrophic (WR 0–20%); hours 12/18/20 are excellent (WR 67–89%). The −$0.47 → +$1.37 swing yesterday was the bad/good-hours mix shifting. SOL is not structurally positive or negative.

Yesterday afternoon: rule× compressed to +0.09× (15:00 UTC window). Overnight: rebounded to +0.59× → +0.71×. Still below 1.0× the whole time — the rule kept 45–71% of baseline P&L but never amplified it.

Q6 · Head-fake pattern repeated · framing misleading

L-streaks preceded by exactly 1 W: lifetime 51.7% · yesterday afternoon 28.6% · overnight 66.7% · last-100 69.2%. The 61% pattern repeated and intensified. But: P(L | prev=W) overnight = 65.6% vs base P(L) = 75.6% — prev-W still reduces loss probability by ~10pp. "Most L-streaks start with 1 W" is true but doesn't mean "after 1 W you're more likely to lose." A skip-after-W rule remains subtractive.
