Streak structure (sim20, 171 trades)

Sim20's wins and losses don't behave like independent flips. The conditional probabilities tell the story:

That's a −34pp anti-streak gap — but in the opposite direction from what most intuition suggests. Wins cluster with wins; losses cluster with losses. The mechanism is intra-window correlation: the bot trades 5 assets in parallel, and when crypto is broadly stable the cheap side hits everywhere, when crypto is trending hard the cheap side misses everywhere.

Longest win streak: 6 (independence baseline ≈ 1.4)
Longest loss streak: 12 (independence baseline ≈ 3.4)
Total flips (W↔L): 46 over 170 adjacent pairs (27%)
Average flip cadence: 3.1/hour — one flip every ~20 min

What precedes each streak (the asymmetry)

Loss streaks → head-fakes

14 of 23 (61%) of all loss streaks follow exactly one isolated win. Long loss streaks (≥5) are even more lopsided: 9 of 12 follow a single W.

...LLLLLLLW | LLLLLLLLLL ← 9-L streak after 1 W
...LLLLLLLW | LLLLLLLLL ← head-fake pattern repeats

Win streaks → droughts

6 of 8 win streaks of length ≥3 emerge from a long loss streak (≥4). Wins need momentum to build — the regime change is preceded by a longer drought.

...LLLLLLLLL | WWWWWW ← W×6 after 9-L
...LLLLLLLLLLL | WWWW ← W×4 after 11-L

On/off rule backtest

Rule: flip v2 ON after a sim20 W, flip OFF after a sim20 L. Manual operation via the Sim20 Signal panel in the v2 dashboard.

Rule variant Trades taken WR P&L ($5 bets) P&L ($2 bets)
Always trade (baseline)17129.2%+$142.18+$56.87
Flip ON after 1 W4953.1%+$282.96+$113.18
Flip ON after 2 W2556.0%+$156.13+$62.45
Flip ON after 3 W1442.9%+$47.83+$19.13

Why the 1-W variant wins: waiting for 2 wins skips legitimate gains from 2-streaks (4 of 24 W-streaks are length 2). The extra wins from those streaks outweigh the head-fake losses you eat with the 1-W rule.

SOL handling: SOL stays in sim20's signal feed even though it's disabled in v2 trades — its W/L votes help regime detection on the other 4 assets. Excluding SOL from the signal entirely would cost ~$26 at $2 bets.

⚠ Caveat: this is in-sample on a single 15-hour day. Out-of-sample performance will be smaller; how much is the open question for May 12+.
